ℹ️ How Paper Generation Works

Algorithmic workflow for creating non-conflicting exam sets
1
Weighted Pool Balance: Questions are drawn evenly across your selected topic pools. The generator prioritizes least-used questions first to ensure even question coverage and zero internal duplicates per set.
2
Independent Randomization: The question order is shuffled for each paper. For objective (MCQ) quizzes, all ABCD choices within every question are independently scrambled.
3
Unique Paper UID: A distinct paper tracking code (e.g. CD-2026-1) is stamped on each student sheet and embedded into the Master Answer Key index.
4
Rapid TA Evaluation: Post-exam, TAs grade student responses instantly via the Rapid Numpad grading screen or through batch Excel answer uploads.
